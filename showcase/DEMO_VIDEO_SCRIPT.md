# LectureLens Demo Video Script

Target runtime: 4 to 5 minutes

## Scenes

1. **Opening** - Introduce the accessibility problem and the LectureLens promise.
2. **Bring material** - Show the upload and paste-text entry points.
3. **Choose a learning mode** - Explain Explainer, Revision, and Deep dive.
4. **Create the lesson** - Show the guided processing flow and offline fallback.
5. **Listen and replay** - Demonstrate narration, voice, speed, paragraph replay, and progress.
6. **Deck map** - Show the three-slide structure and independent slide playback.
7. **Study kit and tutor** - Demonstrate quiz, flashcards, next steps, and grounded questions.
8. **Close** - Summarize the value, architecture, and next product horizon.

The MP4 is generated from browser captures of the running app. Re-capture the three PNGs with the local browser and run `build_demo_video.py` to refresh it.
